Robin Fischer

PhD Researcher

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at WU Vienna, hosted at the Complexity Science Hub. My research focuses on technological change: how firms learn and compete as new technologies emerge. My broader interests include productivity, causal inference, and forecasting.

Previously, I earned an M.Sc. in Economics from the University of Cologne and a B.A. in Mathematics and Philosophy from TU Braunschweig.
